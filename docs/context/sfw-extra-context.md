## The organisation

- The Soil Food Web Foundation is a US nonprofit, registered with the Oregon DOJ, with its own board and IRS determination letter.
- Its school runs on Thinkific (school.soilfoodweb.com) and WordPress/WooCommerce (soilfoodweb.com). The old LifterLMS platform moved to archive.soilfoodweb.com.
- About 10,000 students worldwide, and about 50,000 Instagram followers (@soilfoodwebschool).
- Workshops run with a local partner in each country: Costa Rica, England (Wild Ken Hill), India, Santa Fe / New Mexico, Yucatán.
- Partner organisations in the UK and India. India is central to the mission; Evan presented workshop decks to Vandana Shiva's network in Oct 2026.
- Products and tools:
  - sMApp, a soil map app with student and directory maps (Linnea built the bioregion toggle on those maps)
  - a free Public Community
  - Givebutter for donations
  - Ontraport for autoresponders
  - monday.com boards (Summer Sprint, Student Support, CRM, Website Update)
  - Later.com for scheduling
- Brand:
  - fonts: Montserrat, Source Sans 3, EB Garamond
  - colours: Soil Brown #4F3433, Food Web Green #156826, Harvest Gold #C9A227, Organic Cream #F4F1EA, Education Blue #3780B8
- Direction from the Executive Director: a community brand, less of a personal brand around Dr. Elaine Ingham.
- Big fundraising campaign launches 1 Dec 2026 (Giving Tuesday).

## Extra proof points (all usable in applications)

**Scale and data**
- Manages 7.34 TB of raw SFW footage, heading toward 10 TB.
- Built a faceted asset library with provenance tracking: who took each asset, where it came from and a link back to its source. It pairs with an AI content pipeline and runs workers on Railway with Cloudflare R2 storage.

**Product and tooling**
- **Wormhole:** a password-protected post planner. The team scrolls mock Instagram and LinkedIn feeds, and the editor changes text live. Built with Claude Code and the Claude API.
- **SFW Reel Studio:** a CapCut-style Reel editor with an AI chat panel, Remotion motion graphics and ElevenLabs voiceover.
- **Learning games** built on SFW course material:
  - DirtKitchen: brewing compost extract and tea, Papa's Pizzeria style
  - Oregon Orchard: permaculture orchards
  - Microcosm (planned): re-running Dr. Ingham's soil experiments in a jar
  - All are pitched as course add-ons, with scientific sources shown.
- **Interactive map** of Dr. Elaine Ingham's publications, with Google Scholar links and "Useful for" summaries.
- **Pitched an internal local AI** that makes Dr. Ingham's books searchable and keeps data in-house.
- **Pitched a soil-data partnership:** connecting sMApp to open soil DNA data (BeOpen Atlas) as a revenue line.

**Process wins**
- **Webinar-to-Shorts flow**, which the Executive Director loved:
  - the full recording goes on YouTube
  - 4 Shorts are cut that week
  - the best Short opens the recording as a hook
- **One Later post per Short:** it publishes to YouTube, Instagram and Facebook at once, 4 times a week, starting 24 Nov 2026.
- **Shorts thumbnails:** designed for the whole backlog in one format and 4 colours.
- **Grant pipeline:**
  - a web scraper finds grants, then every link, rule and fit is checked by hand
  - the tracker separates grants SFW leads from grants to share with the community
  - each application is a Google Doc with an Application tab and a Workflow tab (link, deadline, requirements, owner, status)
  - SAM.gov grants were dropped for 2 months after a paperwork review
- **Merch store:**
  - Shopify plus Printful, global shipping
  - designed as a lifestyle brand
  - own product photoshoot on natural backgrounds
  - a UBI (unrelated business income) memo for legal review
  - built in about 2 weeks, starting from a 4-day ask
- **Workshop pages:** removed two workshops with no record of happening, so every claim on the site has a source.
- **Fundraising documents:** 4 redesigned for donors, keeping the Executive Director's text 100% exact.
